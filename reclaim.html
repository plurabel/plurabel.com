Reclaim

Send these to the group chat, the school, the reporter — without leaking where you live. Clean copies; your originals stay untouched.

What it does

  • Pick photos or videos from your library or Files; clean copies go back to Photos or onward through the share sheet.
  • “Reclaim — strip metadata” sits on the system share sheet, so the fix happens before you send.
  • Strips GPS location, camera serials, and owner/creator names. Orientation survives; pixels are untouched or re-encoded once, depending on format.
  • JPEG, PNG, HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, WebP, MOV/MP4/M4V. Anything else is listed as skipped, never mangled — a corrupt file fails loudly, an animated GIF is refused rather than flattened to one still.
  • Never overwrites. The file you pick is the file you keep.

What it won’t do

  • Upload your photos anywhere — stripping runs on the phone, no network at all.
  • Edit pixels: it removes hidden data, not faces.
  • Keep a Live Photo whole — the still+clip pairing lives in the metadata and goes with it. You get the honest still, not a broken pair.
  • Charge you again.

Pick, strip, save — three taps on the phone.
