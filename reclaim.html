Reclaim

Send these to the group chat, the school, the reporter — without leaking where you live. Safe-to-share copies; your originals stay untouched.

What it does

  • Drop photos, screenshots, or a whole folder; cleaned copies land in reclaimed/.
  • Strips GPS location, camera serial numbers, and owner/creator names by default.
  • JPEG, PNG, HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, WebP, MOV/MP4/M4V. Anything else is listed in failed.txt, not mangled.
  • Never overwrites. The file you drop is the file you keep.

What it won’t do

  • Upload your photos anywhere — the cleaning runs locally.
  • Edit pixels: it removes hidden data, not faces.
  • Charge you again.
