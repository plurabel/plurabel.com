Plurabel

Support

Plurabel is four iPhone apps. Each one does a single job on files you already own — mostly from the system share sheet, so the fix happens where the file landed.

  • Plurabel Reveal — scans and image-only PDFs become searchable PDFs. The original stays.
  • Plurabel Recite — an EPUB you own becomes an m4b audiobook with chapters. Uses the speech voices installed on your phone.
  • Plurabel Reclaim — copies in reclaimed/ with GPS, serial, and owner tags stripped by default.
  • Plurabel Reprint — HEIC/HEIF to JPEG in jpeg/. Live Photo movies are skipped on purpose.

Failures write failed.txt next to the outputs. If a file is locked (DRM EPUB) or the engine is missing, that is a line in that file, not a crash.

The apps are heading to the App Store; this page will be the support URL on the store records.
