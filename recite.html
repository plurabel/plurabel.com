Recite

An EPUB you own becomes an audiobook — an m4b with chapters — read by a voice already on your phone.

What it does

  • Open an EPUB you own — from Files or the share sheet — and get an m4b audiobook with chapters.
  • Pick any voice installed on the phone; the ones in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content just work.
  • Everything is synthesized on the device; the finished book sits in the app’s folder, visible in Files.

What it won’t do

  • Read DRM-locked ebooks — a locked book is reported, not cracked.
  • Record your own voice or clone one.
  • Send the text anywhere — no network.
  • Charge you again.

Pick a book, pick a voice, get an audiobook.
