Recite

An EPUB you own becomes a local audiobook — chapter WAV files and a single m4b — read by a voice already on your Mac.

What it does

  • Drop an EPUB you own; get chapter audio plus an m4b audiobook.
  • Uses macOS’s built-in speech voices — the ones in System Settings. Pick another installed voice and Recite uses it.
  • Everything is synthesized on this machine; nothing is sent away.

What it won’t do

  • Read DRM-locked ebooks — a locked file is a line in failed.txt, not a workaround.
  • Record your own voice or clone one.
  • Charge you again.
