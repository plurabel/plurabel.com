Privacy

The four Plurabel apps run on your iPhone. They read files you pick and write clean copies to Photos or Files — the phone itself does the work; your documents, photos, and ebooks never leave it.

There is no account and no analytics SDK. Reveal’s OCR, Reclaim’s metadata cleanup, Recite’s speech, and Reprint’s conversion all run on-device — the apps don’t use the network at all.

This site itself is a static page. If a future purchase form or mailing list is added, that page will say so here before it collects anything.
