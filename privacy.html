Privacy

The four Plurabel apps run on your Mac. They read files you drop and write files beside them (or in a folder you pick). They do not send your documents, photos, or ebooks to Plurabel.

There is no account. There is no analytics SDK in the apps. Everything runs locally — Reveal’s OCR, Reclaim’s metadata cleanup, Recite’s voices, and Reprint’s conversion all happen on your Mac.

This site itself is a static page. If a future purchase form or mailing list is added, that page will say so here before it collects anything.
