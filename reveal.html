Plurabel

Reveal

A folder of scans becomes searchable, selectable PDFs. OCR runs on your Mac — the pages never leave it.

What it does

  • Drop a folder of scans or image-only PDFs; searchable copies land beside them.
  • Originals are never replaced — output is a new file.
  • English OCR via the industry-standard engine; no cloud service, no account.
  • Pages it can’t read are listed in failed.txt, not silently dropped.

What it won’t do

  • Upload your documents anywhere.
  • Rewrite or compress the originals.
  • Charge you again.
